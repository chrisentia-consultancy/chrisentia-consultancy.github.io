Efficiency and sustainability are the same problem

Automation that reduces waste, energy use and rework moves a plant toward its production targets and its sustainability commitments at the same time. Chrisentia builds systems that measure both, so progress against the UN Sustainable Development Goals is evidenced rather than estimated.

  • Less material waste through vision-based inspection and counting
  • Lower energy and water use through monitored, demand-matched operation
  • Measurable baselines and trends, ready for reporting
